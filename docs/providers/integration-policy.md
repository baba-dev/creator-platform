# Provider integration policy

BytePlus is the only customer-facing media generation provider. NVIDIA is used
for creative reasoning, prompt refinement, storyline generation, localization,
and structured planning.

Do not enable a model until the team has verified:

- the exact provider model identifier and region;
- input, output, duration, resolution, and size constraints;
- provider unit pricing and the company's negotiated discount;
- asynchronous status and cancellation behavior;
- moderation, retention, and data-processing rules;
- resale or embedded-platform permission; and
- whether the capability is activated on the company account.

Provider usage is treated as billable. A BytePlus credit limit is not modeled as
free platform credit.
