Keep shaping the idea.

This lightweight guide is included with the installed app and needs no connection. It contains no personal data or generated media.

Image direction

Describe the subject and action, composition, camera distance, lighting, mood, palette and intended aspect ratio. Start with a clear brief and iterate deliberately.

Video planning

Define the opening frame, camera motion, subject movement, transition and end frame. Break longer sequences into smaller shots and maintain consistent visual continuity.

Voice and scripts

Write for listening: shorter sentences, clear pronunciation cues, natural pauses and an audience-appropriate tone. Review long-form scripts in sections.

When you're back online

Review the estimate and parameters before submitting a generation. Copy your locally encrypted draft into the appropriate studio only when you choose to do so.
